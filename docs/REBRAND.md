## Direction decision

The owner delegated selection and pinned the light operating atlas, ultramarine accent, warm paper, Noto Sans and CQ geometry. Impeccable 4.3.1 context and concept seed ran before UI changes; seed `0ba7a8ea`, assigned index 7. The pinned brief takes precedence over the random assignment. No additional owner approval is required.

Grounded structures considered: (1) professional development dossier; (2) editorial learning journal; (3) competency matrix; (4) advisor casebook; (5) training timetable; (6) decision ledger; (7) working atlas of competencies. The atlas carries the product mechanism across a personal next step, prerequisite route and review queue. Dossier is familiar but makes the next action secondary. Matrix scales to HR but overloads an employee's first view. Atlas is selected with readable tables for dense tasks, not a pictorial map.

The dealt spectrogram, laboratory counter, cassette inlay, dance notation, cloud spectrum and transforming textile were compared against the brief. Their literal visual systems lose audience identification and product clarity here: dark instrumentation conflicts with the pinned light palette; cassette/dance/cloth interactions add unfamiliar navigation; cloud effects do not explain competency evidence. Retained disciplines: shared alignment rails, stable numeric positions, visible effort budgets, precise sequence, restrained color and progressive disclosure. No decorative skins or borrowed imagery are used.

First-view compositions: a balanced profile dashboard, a full competency matrix and a next-action-led atlas. Select the third: compact identity/goal, mandatory lane, actionable recommendation, with competency detail below and a contextual side column on desktop. On mobile use the same reading order. The three implementation pilots are employee path, practical-project approval and HR analytics/import.
